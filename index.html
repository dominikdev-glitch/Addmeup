AddMeUp — Social Handle Exchange Platform for Snapchat & WhatsApp

AddMeUp is a social discovery and handle exchange platform where everyday users, creators, and businesses share their Snapchat handles and WhatsApp numbers to grow their audience, earn promotional points, and connect with real community members.

How AddMeUp Uses Google Sign-In Data

AddMeUp uses Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0) solely to authenticate your account identity, retrieve your basic profile (name, email address, and profile photo), and secure your promotional points wallet. We never sell or share your personal data with third parties.
